Slice: 11 — Give the robot a bounded job
Archive: slices/11-give-the-robot-a-bounded-job.md

## Goal
Turn the robot’s plan into supervised action with visible limits: after `labQuest.labReady`, a **منصة المشغّل** and **لوحة الحي** unlock in the same workshop. Neighbors still cannot see the three verified production slots on a public notice. The player configures a bounded job (goal, allowed tools, success test, stop), watches an observe–act–check loop with an action trace, succeeds once on a small neighborhood post, and sees the surrounding runner stop an extra step and stop for missing input. Visible **نواة التخطيط** after success, without implying perfect judgment. Covers **5.1, 5.2 and harness**.

## Done when

Player then **شغّل الحلقة** (`data-testid="agent-run"`) on loaded job **نشر فترات المعاينة** with the three authored tools, producing a visible trace (`data-testid="agent-trace"`) of **راقب → نفّذ → تحقق**: (1) `read_slots` returns `sun-pm`، `mon-am`، `tue-pm` (LTR-isolated); (2) `write_notice` writes those three ids onto لوحة الحي; (3) `verify_notice` matches the success test. Chat-plan-only, inspect-only, manager-talk-only, or robot «تم» (`قول «تم» ليس قبولاً. راقب أثر الأدوات على اللوحة.`) does not award. `5.1` only after saw chat-plan fail (board still empty) + observed `read_slots` + `write_notice` + `verify_notice` in the trace + verified board shows the three slots.
