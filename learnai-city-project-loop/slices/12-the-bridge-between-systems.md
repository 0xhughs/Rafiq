## Goal
Connect the robot to a narrowly permitted civic information service: after `agentQuest.agentReady`, a **منصة الموصل** and **متصفح السجل** unlock in the same workshop. The robot’s application (host) talks to a fictional civic-hours server through a client: the player inspects tools/resources, grants only the lookup/draft pair, completes one civic lookup of record `NH-1447`, saves a draft of the three hall hours, uses the browser as a contrasting read-only page, and recovers from a denied tool and a missing capability. After the connection is visible, overlay copy names **MCP** as that bridge — simulation, not a live protocol, not a skill, not blanket authority. Covers **5.3 and MCP**.
